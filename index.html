    How it works

    Vestafi had 1,354 applications in year one and fewer than half went on to contribute (founder's letter, May 2026). A person reads every one. This desk makes that reading fast, so the reply goes out the same day.

    1. An application landsFrom the apply form or the WhatsApp inbox you already run.
    2. It gets read into fixed fieldsCity, budget, way in, worries, readiness, and how sure it is. Here: rules in your browser. Live: a small model at temperature 0, returning a strict schema.
    3. A reply is drafted from your own termsOnly sentences from Vestafi's documents. A guard blocks return figures and anything not on file.
    4. A person approves, edits or takes overTax, legal, large tickets and trust challenges go straight to a human.
    5. Sent and followed upOut on WhatsApp, logged against the applicant, with a nudge before Thursday's call.

    What to watch: the share of applicants who contribute, and hours from application to first reply.
